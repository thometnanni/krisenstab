Footage sourced from social networks can become crucial working material for investigative journalists, OSINT investigators and human rights activists. Yet the high persuasive power videos have, demands rigorous verification: Do place and time of a recording actually match the described incident? What is actually shown in the imagery? And how do recordings from different perspectives relate to each other?

In [Vantage](https://vantage.thometnanni.net/) we pursue video and still image based spatial reconstruction to aid in answering these questions. Supported by the Prototype Fund we created a [platform](https://github.com/thometnanni/vantage) to reproject footage into 3D-environments and are currently [exploring a wider set of reconstruction techniques](https://github.com/orgs/v-a-n-t-a-g-e/repositories).
