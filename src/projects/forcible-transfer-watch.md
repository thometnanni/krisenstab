_Forcible Transfer Watch_ monitors and documents the risk of forcible transfer affecting over 200 Palestinian communities in Area C of the occupied West Bank. It does so within a context of escalating coercion by Israeli authorities and settlement expansion. These pressures create sustained conditions that push Palestinian communities to leave their land.

We developed the interface together with the _West Bank Protection Consortium_, the Israeli anti-settlement _NGO Kerem Navot_, and the civilian harm watchdog _Airwars_.
