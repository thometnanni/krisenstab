Teaching at Weißensee Kunsthochschule

We’ve been teaching at the summer and winter semester introducing creative coding and contemporary web design to students of the Weißensee Kunsthochschule in Berlin. Across weekly sessions we covered p5.js, web fundamentals, publishing workflows, and the cultural history of the web through examples, timelines and references. Students developed small interactive prototypes and personal web pages which were published online at the end of the semester.
